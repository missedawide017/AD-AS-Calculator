STANDARD AD-AS RULES: AD is downward sloping; a rightward AD shift raises both output and the price level. SRAS is upward sloping; a left/up SRAS shift lowers output and raises the price level, while a right/down shift raises output and lowers the price level. LRAS is vertical at potential GDP. The intersection of AD and SRAS gives the short-run equilibrium; comparing that output with LRAS gives the recessionary or inflationary gap.
